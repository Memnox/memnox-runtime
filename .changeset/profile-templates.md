A catalog of seven job descriptions ships with the runtime: Software Development, Code Review, Testing, Documentation, Research, Customer Support and Operations Assistant. `memnox profile templates` lists them and `memnox profile from-template <template>` copies one into a new profile, taking only the suggested skills named with `--skills`; suggested rules are shown as sentences and nothing applies on its own. The daemon answers `templates` and `profile-from-template`.
