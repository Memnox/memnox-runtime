A working agent reaches the person's connected apps through Memnox with two tools on the `memnox-session` server, `apps` and `app_run`, rather than holding any credential of its own. A read the rules allow runs at once; anything the rules ask about, and every act, waits on the Mac as a work card marked `agentAsked` until the person presses Send and confirms with Touch ID, and a deny refuses it. Each call is a ledger row by name as `app.<toolkit>.<tool>`, and the connections list now says which agents used each app lately in `usedBy`. What an agent read through an app or through one of its MCP servers is kept, redacted and capped, in the person's own memory on this machine as an `app-calls` or `mcp-calls` source, read by the same extractor as their notes into Learn as that agent's candidates, with each piece of evidence naming its call in `call`. None of it is synced or uploaded, personal sync included.
