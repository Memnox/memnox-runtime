`memnox sessions` lists every session from the last day in groups by what it needs from the person: Needs you, Stopped, Working, Finished and Idle, with what needs a person first and a count beside each group. These are the same statuses and reasons the apps show.

`memnox approvals` now lists everything waiting on the person, both here and what the workspace routed to them, and `memnox inbox` is another name for it rather than a second command.
